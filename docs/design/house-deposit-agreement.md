# House deposit: fixed-term concept agreement

Sam’s illustrative terms now model a 75-month term from 1 January 2025 to
1 April 2031, at 5.1% AER fixed. This is an authored prototype scenario, not
an HSBC product offer or a reconstruction of real payment history.

The existing £6,400 contribution balance represents 20 £320 instalments.
The authored opening £6,080 represents 19 instalments before August 2026;
the recorded August receipt is instalment 20. This saving history predates
Sam’s Atlas tenure and the March 2026 creation of the pot in Atlas. Later
qualifying receipts update progress; interest, scheduled instructions and
future-dated receipts do not. Partial receipts fill the next instalment.

The £24,000 cap applies to the contribution balance. Interest is held separately
and paid at maturity, so the customer receives more than the cap without reducing
their contribution allowance. The illustration assumes 75 month-end payments,
monthly compounding equivalent to 5.1% AER, and no missed payments. It gives
£24,000 contributions + £4,090.98 interest = £28,090.98, displayed in whole pounds.
It does not claim accrued interest is already in the ledger or net worth.
Payment timing changes the actual interest; no invented missed-payment penalty applies.

Withdrawals, outgoing rules and evolution into an investment pot are blocked
until maturity. Manual deposits cannot breach the cap, rule execution is limited
to the available room, and Future contribution projections use the same cap.
The illustration is in the agreement; it does not silently credit projected
interest into the prototype ledger or the existing Future forecast.

The mobile layout gives the maturity breakdown a single tinted card, followed
by 75 dots and explicit completed/remaining labels. Assumptions are expandable.
Both the short agreement and full terms use the same calculation and visual.
