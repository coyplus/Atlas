# Future: planned paths and possibilities

The canvas combines two deliberately different kinds of information. Solid, balance-sized circles are actual or explicitly previewed Pots. Soft grey bubbles carry short, meaningful names such as “Shorter weeks” and “Time away”. They are invitations to imagine, with no monetary meaning to their size. Target rings retain the dotted outline around a solid balance. Solid bubbles have no outlines; a Reached label identifies completion while personal colours and photos remain recognisable.

All suggested possibilities appear along the timeline as soft grey plus markers. Only the two nearest to the viewed date appear on the canvas. Markers open an explanation rather than changing the slider: they locate a life-stage prompt, not a forecast completion date. One Make it mine page uses the short suggestion name as its editable title, keeps the full personal reasoning and approximate date, and shows prefilled target and contribution fields that are optional to adjust. Its two decisions stay in a footer at the bottom of the detail screen; on short screens only the supporting content scrolls. Make it real creates only that Pot and monthly Rule, with Undo; unrelated What If experiments remain drafts. Not for me only hides the suggestion. Neither discovering nor dismissing a possibility changes a balance, Money Speed, forecast or membership status.

The prototype's AI is deterministic and local. Long-range prompts change by persona, viewed age, family context and the nearest chapter. A newly selected experiment takes precedence, then travelling to another chapter restores contextual discovery. Incomplete unfunded goals are acknowledged rather than described as completed. Life-stage inspiration is explicitly illustrative: there are no peer statistics, benchmarks, inferred family events or assumed life choices.

## Pot identity

The shared Pot detail displays its current colour as a named swatch. Expanding it reveals an inline palette; selecting a colour immediately updates the detail, canvas and timeline without moving the camera or resetting scroll. The separate Photo control opens a preview where a locally uploaded JPEG, PNG or WebP can be saved or cancelled. Primary navigation disappears in Pot details; the content uses the full screen and the final activity remains reachable. Files decode locally, are downsized, and are stored with the demo on the device; no upload service is involved. The detail uses a diagonal fade behind its content. Bubble photos receive a dark blue scrim that keeps white type legible even on light photos. Initial examples reuse existing assets: Sam's family holiday (horizon landscape), his friends Pot (cooking), and Elena's family holiday (her supplied family photo). These are visual examples, not evidence of a booked trip or future intention.

Pinch and keyboard zoom run from 0.4× to 32×; Fit reframes the visible field. Text size stays fixed and labels appear only when the circle has enough room. Ghost hit areas follow their visible circles so they cannot intercept tiny neighbouring Pots.
